Steal a Brainrot

A multiplayer game built around collecting and stealing characters inspired by Italian brainrot memes.

Overview

Steal a Brainrot is a 2025 Roblox game about acquiring the block-like “Brainrot” characters based on the Italian brainrot meme. Its central idea turns the characters readers see in the Italian brainrot article into a collecting and stealing game.

The game became widely discussed through social-media clips of players gaining rare characters or losing them to other players.

Why this article is here

You arrived from Trippi Troppi, one of the characters described in the Italian brainrot article. This page shows another way the broader character trend entered popular culture.

Gameplay

Players begin with a base and some in-game currency. They can buy Brainrot characters from a conveyor belt, and those characters generate more currency over time. Players can also try to steal characters from other bases while defending their own collections.

Equipment and temporary shields help players protect a base or attempt a theft. Special events introduce more characters and give returning players new goals.

Popularity

The game attracted large audiences on Roblox in 2025. Clips about stealing characters, defending bases, and reacting to losses helped it spread on TikTok and YouTube.

Licensing and partnerships

The game’s success led to a licensed version in Fortnite Creative. Use of some Italian brainrot characters also raised questions about who could license or control the original character designs.

Reception

Writers described the collecting-and-stealing loop as easy to understand and hard to put down. Others criticized paid advantages and the frustration of losing a valuable character. The game won Best Creative Direction at the 2025 Roblox Innovation Awards.
